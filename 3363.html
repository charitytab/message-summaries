Testimony

Minister: Brother William Branham
Date Preached: Wednesday, May 9, 1951 (evening)
Location: Los Angeles, California
Reference: 51-0509

Summary

A testimony is not merely a personal story — it is a declaration of what God has done, a weapon against unbelief, and an open invitation for the next person to believe; and the cascade of testimonies filling this final Los Angeles service, from an 84-year-old man who wants to scatter the Message to a former United States Congressman healed after 66 years of crippling, announces one unchanging truth: you cannot figure God out, but you can believe Him, and He will not fail.

Scripture Foundation

  • Revelation 12:11 — "And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony" — the scriptural authority for the role of testimony in spiritual warfare and in building faith.
  • John 4:39–42 — The Samaritan woman's testimony that drew the whole city to Jesus: a single person's witness producing corporate faith and a corporate encounter with Christ.

Also referenced: Psalm 66:16 ("Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will declare what he hath done for my soul"); Mark 5:18–20 (the Gadarene demoniac sent back to testify — "Go home to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee"); Hebrews 11 (the great hall of faith — each name a testimony of what God did for someone who dared to believe Him).

Main Points

  • An elderly man with nothing but enthusiasm is still a minister of the Gospel. Brother Branham opened this service by honoring an 84-year-old man who could no longer work but burned with a desire to scatter the Message of Jesus Christ. Brother Branham took up a personal offering to help him do it: "If he has the enthusiasm to scatter the Message of Jesus Christ, I want to help him do it." Age and circumstance cannot extinguish the flame of a testimony that must be told.
  • A former Congressman healed after 66 years — the testimony that silenced the skeptics. Among the extraordinary healings of the campaign, Brother Branham highlighted the case of a former United States Congressman who had been crippled for sixty-six years. He had come to the meeting, he had been prayed for, and now he stood before the congregation as living proof. This was not a quiet, private healing — it was a public, documented, government-credentialed witness to the power of Jesus Christ.
  • Simple, childlike faith accomplishes what sophisticated reasoning cannot. Brother Branham recounted the story of a large man who carried his elderly father through the rain to get to the prayer line. Asked if he was afraid of pneumonia, the man answered without hesitation: "Jesus Christ is take care of me. I bring my daddy." Brother Branham said: "Simple child-like faith to believe." That kind of unargued, unanalyzed trust in the Person of Christ is precisely what releases miracles.
  • You cannot figure God out — and you are not supposed to. The running thread through the evening was the futility of trying to reason your way to a miracle. "There's where you lose. You can't figure God out. You got to believe Him." The intellect that insists on understanding the mechanism before it will trust the promise will wait forever. Faith does not require a logical pathway — it requires a Person and His Word.
  • Testimony is the legacy of every healed person. Every person who came through the prayer line during this campaign left with a responsibility: to tell what God had done. Not to be silent, not to keep it private, but to stand before their family, their community, and their skeptics, and declare: "He healed me." That testimony, spoken in faith, would carry the same power to build faith in the next generation that the testimonies of this week had carried to everyone in the room.

Quote

"Jesus Christ is take care of me. I bring my daddy."

These words — spoken by a large man soaking wet from carrying his elderly father through the rain to reach the prayer line — became the defining image of the message. Brother Branham held it up as the purest expression of the faith that moves mountains: no theology, no argument, no conditions — just an uncomplicated certainty that Jesus would handle everything else if the person would simply come. "See, we're very suspicious. And we going to try to look in there and see if we can figure it out. There's where you lose."

Final Exhortation

Before you leave this meeting, make sure you have a testimony — and then make sure you tell it. God did not heal you for your own private benefit; He healed you so that someone else, hearing what He did for you, would dare to believe He can do it for them. And if you are still waiting for your healing tonight, take the posture of that man in the rain: stop trying to figure God out, stop weighing the odds, stop asking "how?" — just come through the rain, bring your need, and say with absolute certainty, "Jesus Christ is take care of me." That is the faith that gets the answer.
