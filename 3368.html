My Commission

Minister: Brother William Branham
Date Preached: Saturday, May 5, 1951 (evening)
Location: Los Angeles, California
Reference: 51-0505

Summary

Brother Branham's healing commission was not self-appointed or denominationally conferred — it was given by a divine messenger in 1946, and the gift of discernment that operates through him has been progressively refined by God from physical contact to pure vision, so that no one would confuse the man with the Presence, and so that the God who reveals secret things would be glorified alone.

Scripture Foundation

  • Judges 6:11–14 — Gideon commissioned by the Angel of the Lord: the biblical pattern of God selecting and equipping a human vessel through a supernatural visitation.
  • Luke 1:19 — Gabriel's words to Zacharias: "I am Gabriel, that stand in the presence of God; and am sent to speak unto thee" — the pattern of an angelic commission authenticating a divine ministry.

Also referenced: Numbers 12:6 ("If there be a prophet among you, I the Lord will make myself known unto him in a vision"); 1 Samuel 3:1–10 (Samuel's call and the progressive recognition of a divine voice); Acts 10:38 ("God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil"); John 5:19 ("The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do").

Main Points

  • The commission came through a heavenly messenger. In 1946, Brother Branham received a visitation from an Angel who told him he was given a gift of healing for the people and that, if he would get the people to believe, nothing would stand before the prayer. This was not something he sought or manufactured — it was sovereignly given, and his entire ministry rested on that initial moment of commissioning.
  • The gift began through physical contact and matured to pure vision. In the early years, the gift operated when Brother Branham held a person's hand — a vibration or sign in the palm would indicate disease. As the ministry developed, God refined the gift to seeing visions directly, without any physical contact, so that the supernatural nature of the revelation would be unmistakable and leave no room for the accusation that the man was simply reading physical signs.
  • God refined the gift to remove every possibility of human credit. Brother Branham explained that God deliberately changed the mode of operation because the earlier method could cause confusion — people might think he was physically feeling something rather than receiving a supernatural revelation. The progression was God's doing, not Brother Branham's engineering: "I can do nothing within myself, only what the Lord shows me."
  • The visions show what will come to pass — with perfect accuracy. Brother Branham recounted specific instances where visions had shown future events — a blind woman's sight restored, a deaf woman healed at home after the meeting — all of which came to pass exactly as seen. He did not offer these as boasts but as authentication: the same God who showed it in the vision brought it to pass in reality.
  • Critics do not disprove the gift — they are invited to examine it. To those who accused him of being a faker, Brother Branham gave a simple challenge: "Come prove that I'm wrong." He held no resentment toward skeptics and acknowledged that he himself was nothing — just a servant who yielded to what the Lord showed him.

Quote

"I can do nothing within myself, only what the Lord shows me to do. I can't do no more than He shows me to do. And in these visions, He shows me things that will come to pass, shows me things that people has done."

This was Brother Branham's consistent testimony throughout his ministry: the discernment did not originate with him, could not be turned on or off by him, and was not subject to his will. He was purely a yielded vessel. The commission placed a divine gift through him — but the Source, the intelligence, and the power behind every vision was God alone.

Final Exhortation

Understand that the gift you are witnessing in this campaign was not produced by a man — it was given by God, refined by God, and authenticated by God through signs that no human being could manufacture. When you come before the Lord in prayer tonight, do not come looking at the servant; come looking at the One who sent him. Your healing is not in Brother Branham's hand — it is in the nail-scarred hands of Jesus Christ. And what He has commissioned, He is faithful to perform. Come with open hands and an open heart, and receive what God has already shown is yours.
