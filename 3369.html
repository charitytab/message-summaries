As I Was With Moses

Minister: Brother William Branham
Date Preached: Thursday, May 3, 1951 (evening)
Location: Los Angeles, California
Reference: 51-0503

Summary

The same God who promised Joshua "as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee" is the God who stands behind every healing campaign today — His authority over sickness has not diminished, His Word has not grown weak, and the supernatural signs that accompany this ministry are not for spectacle but to quicken the faith of desperate people until they, like the centurion, can simply say: "Speak the Word, and it shall be done."

Scripture Foundation

  • Joshua 1:5, 9 — "As I was with Moses, so I will be with thee; I will not fail thee nor forsake thee" — the foundational promise that the same God who worked through Moses will work through His servants in every generation.
  • Matthew 8:1–13 — The centurion's faith: "I am not worthy that Thou shouldest come under my roof: but speak the Word only, and my servant shall be healed" — the supreme example of understanding divine authority.
  • Mark 5:21–24, 35–43 — Jairus and his daughter: Jesus meeting a man at his level of faith, going to the house and raising the dead girl, because Jairus needed the physical presence of Jesus to believe.

Also referenced: John 11:21–27 (Martha's faith, "if Thou hadst been here"); Luke 7:1–10 (parallel centurion account); Hebrews 13:8 (Jesus Christ the same yesterday, today, and forever — the theological foundation that the same authority present in the Gospels is present tonight).

Main Points

  • God's promise of presence has no expiration date. As the Lord told Joshua — "as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee" — so He has spoken the same assurance to every generation of His servants. The God who parted the Red Sea, who brought water from the rock, who led Israel by pillar of fire, is the same God present in the healing campaign. His method may vary; His power and faithfulness do not.
  • His written Word is a seed that takes life when believed. Brother Branham declared that the spoken and written Word of Jesus carries the same divine authority tonight as it did in Galilee. "His written Word tonight is a Seed that'll take life as soon as you believe His Word. It's just the same authority tonight over all sickness as It was then." The supernatural signs in the meetings are not a substitute for the Word — they are designed to quicken faith back to the Word.
  • The centurion understood authority; the Church must learn it again. The Roman centurion needed no laying on of hands, no physical sign, no journey to his servant — he simply grasped that the same authority that makes soldiers obey commands also makes sickness obey the command of Christ. "Just speak the Word." Brother Branham held this up as the highest form of faith displayed in the New Testament.
  • Jesus meets people at their level of faith. Jairus, unlike the centurion, could not yet believe by the spoken word alone — he needed Jesus to come to his house. And Jesus went. God does not despise the weaker faith; He accommodates it. The supernatural signs in this campaign are God's way of coming to those who, like Jairus, need something tangible to anchor their believing.
  • Supernatural acts are not the destination — they are a bridge to faith. Every vision, every discernment, every miracle in the prayer line exists for one purpose: to quicken faith in the person watching until they can lay hold of the Word for themselves. "These supernatural acts is to bring your faith back to Him, quicken it right quick — make it quickened to Him."

Quote

"His written Word tonight is a Seed that'll take life as soon as you believe His Word. It's just the same authority tonight over all sickness as It was then. And these supernatural acts is to bring your faith back to Him, quicken it right quick, make it quickened to Him."

This statement captures the entire logic of Brother Branham's healing ministry: the miracles are not ends in themselves but witnesses pointing back to the eternal authority of Christ's Word. The goal is always to move people from dependence on the sign to dependence on the Word — from Jairus's faith to the centurion's faith.

Final Exhortation

You do not need to wait for a sign before you believe — the Word has already been spoken. As the centurion understood that a soldier obeys because of the authority behind the command, understand tonight that sickness must obey the Word of the living Christ. You may come tonight as Jairus came — needing help, needing something to hold onto — and God will meet you there. But press on until you can stand as the centurion stood and say with full assurance: "Just speak the Word, Lord, and I am healed." The same God who was with Moses, who was with Joshua, who walked through the Gospels healing all who came to Him, is with you now — and He will not fail you.
