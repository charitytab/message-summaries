The Angel Of The Lord

Minister: Brother William Branham
Date Preached: Wednesday, May 2, 1951 (evening)
Location: Los Angeles, California
Reference: 51-0502

Summary

The Angel of the Lord who commissioned Brother Branham's healing ministry is not a curiosity or a spectacle — He is the same supernatural Presence that moved through Scripture, confirmed by signs that are beyond human explanation, and His purpose is to make Jesus Christ real and tangible to every believing soul who will stand in faith before Him.

Scripture Foundation

  • Judges 6:11–16 — The Angel of the Lord appearing to Gideon: the same divine Presence that commissioned Old Testament servants now commissioning Brother Branham's ministry.
  • Acts 1:8 — "Ye shall receive power after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you": the promise of supernatural empowerment to the Church.

Also referenced: Malachi 3:1 (the messenger sent before the Lord); John 14:12 ("Greater works than these shall ye do"); Hebrews 13:8 (Jesus Christ the same yesterday, today, and forever — the theological anchor of the entire healing campaign); Acts 2 (Pentecost and the outpouring of the Spirit).

Main Points

  • The Angel is a sign of divine commission, not personal exaltation. Brother Branham carefully explained that when a patient approaches the platform, something supernatural occurs — is not because of the man, but because a divine Presence accompanies the ministry. Those who come in reverence and genuine faith will sense a change in the atmosphere, and the conditions of their life will be disclosed not by human knowledge but by the Spirit of God.
  • Signs exist to produce faith, not to entertain. If a person's condition is revealed — things they never told anyone, things buried deep in their past — the purpose is not to embarrass them but to stir their faith in the living Christ. The supernatural detail is God's way of saying: "I know you, I see you, and I am able to heal you."
  • The Holy Spirit is fire — and fire purifies. The same heavenly fire that burns inbred sin out of a repentant soul can drive out cancer, tumors, and disease. Brother Branham declared that there is nothing mysterious about this: if the Holy Spirit can transform a harlot into a saint and purge the desire of sin from her heart, how much more can that same fire purge sickness from a body presented to God in faith.
  • Holiness and healing belong to the same revival. Referencing the Holiness movement that had shaken the world some forty years prior — when representatives of every religion had gathered and the testimony of transformed lives silenced them all — Brother Branham argued that the God of the camp meeting and the God of the healing line are one and the same. What God began at Azusa He was continuing in Los Angeles.
  • No patient is unknown to the Angel. Whether Brother Branham had met the person before or not, the gift of discernment operated independently of his own knowledge. He warned that the Presence would sometimes speak things the person would not wish disclosed publicly, and that there was nothing he could do to prevent it — it was not his gift to control, only to yield to.

Quote

"He can come down into a human body, drive out the power of cancer, tumor, cataract, tuberculosis with fire, heavenly Holy Spirit Fire falling from God out of heaven. Why, my, that's no mystery. If He can burn inbred sin out, how much more can He burn cancer out by the same Holy Spirit."

This declaration formed the theological spine of the message: the same divine fire that regenerates a sinner's soul is the same power available to heal a sick body. Brother Branham consistently refused to separate salvation and healing as if they operated by different principles — both came from the same Source, through the same Spirit, received by the same faith.

Final Exhortation

Come to this service tonight with all your heart open and yielded. Do not be afraid of what the Spirit might reveal — it is not for your shame but for your faith. If God knows the secret things of your life, then surely He also knows your sickness, and surely He is able to heal it. The Angel of the Lord is present, not as a spectacle for the curious, but as a Healer for the desperate believer. Come reverently, believe with your whole heart, and leave this place tonight knowing that the same God who sent His Angel to commission this ministry has also sent Him to meet your need.
