Exhortation Of Divine Healing

Minister: Brother William Branham
Date Preached: Tuesday, May 1, 1951 (evening)
Location: Los Angeles, California
Reference: 51-0501

Summary

Divine healing is not a method, an emotion, or a preacher's power — it is the sovereign work of the Holy Spirit, received by genuine faith in the Word of God; and just as rain falls on wheat and thistles alike without discriminating, the Spirit can move upon any person, but only a truly born-again heart can lay hold of what God has provided.

Scripture Foundation

  • Hebrews 11:1 — "Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen" (the bedrock text for the entire service).
  • Hebrews 6:7–8 — The ground that drinks in rain but bears thorns and thistles is rejected; used to illustrate that the Holy Spirit falls on all, yet only the born-again bear fruit.
  • Matthew 13:24–30 — The parable of wheat and tares: both grow in the same field under the same rain, but are known by their fruit and separated at harvest.

Also referenced: James 5:14–15 (anointing with oil and the prayer of faith for the sick); John 3:3 ("Ye must be born again"); Numbers 21:8–9 (the brass serpent — Branham describes himself as merely the instrument, not the Healer, "like the brass serpent" that people look through to God).

Main Points

  • A man's word is his bond. Brother Branham opened by explaining that he had been asked to cancel this campaign because another meeting — Brother Freeman's — was already in the city. He refused to break his promise to the people of Los Angeles, declaring that his word, once given, must be kept regardless of the inconvenience.
  • The Holy Spirit falls on all, but salvation requires new birth. Using the illustration of rain in a wheat field that also waters the thistles, Brother Branham warned against mistaking spiritual excitement or a moving of the Spirit for salvation. "Sinners sometimes gets happy and free, but that still doesn't mean they're saved. You have to be borned again to be saved."
  • Divine healing is by faith, not by method. Whether a minister lays hands on the sick, anoints with oil, or prays from a distance, the method is incidental. Faith in the living Word of God is what releases healing — the same Holy Spirit who burns inbred sin out of a heart can drive out cancer and disease by the same fire.
  • The healing gift belongs to God, not to the vessel. Brother Branham took no credit for the discernment operating through him. He pointed the people past himself to the One sending the gift: "If you see the picture back there, you can see my picture too, but I'm not It; It's a separate Person. I'm just — like the brass serpent." The gift placed him in the channel of a prophetic ministry, but all glory belonged to God.
  • Faith is a substance — it is real, and it works. Grounding everything in Hebrews 11:1, Brother Branham exhorted the congregation to come to God not with doubt or a "wait and see" posture, but with the substance of faith already in hand — because faith is not feeling, it is evidence of what the Word has already declared to be true.

Quote

"Sinners sometimes gets happy and free, but that still doesn't mean they're saved. You have to be borned again to be saved."

Brother Branham used this distinction to protect the congregation from mistaking a spiritual atmosphere — in which even unregenerate people can feel something genuine — for the new birth. The rain of the Spirit falls on wheat and thistles alike, but only the wheat brings forth the fruit of true salvation and enduring faith for healing.

"If you see the picture back there, you can see my picture too, but I'm not It; It's a separate Person. But I'm just — like the brass serpent."

Pointing the people away from himself and toward Christ, Brother Branham described his role in the healing ministry the same way the brass serpent in the wilderness functioned: the people did not look at Moses, but through the serpent to God. The gift operated through him, but the Healer was God alone.

Final Exhortation

Come to God tonight not asking whether He can heal, but with the substance of faith already believing that He will — for He has already said it in His Word. Do not let spiritual emotion be mistaken for the new birth, and do not mistake the method of prayer for the source of healing. The same Holy Spirit that washes a sinner clean can drive out every disease. Look past the servant; look to the living Christ who is the same yesterday, today, and forever, and receive what He has already provided for you.
